- Longbridge (asked by the user, who corrected me): an AP (HK/SG) login without a securities account gets a paper account with real-time HK/US/A-share quotes (quote permissions follow the app key, so paper = live; Nasdaq Basic by default). US-region logins have no paper account. OAuth 2.0 with dynamic client registration and a localhost redirect, PKCE-style (`token_endpoint_auth_method: none`).
