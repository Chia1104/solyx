---
title: Reports
description: A view of a listing over quarters, whose claims keep their sources' own words.
---

A report holds the agent's view of a listing over quarters:

- **the stance,** bullish, neutral or bearish, and the thesis behind it;
- **what carries the view,** and **the risks** to it;
- **what would change the view,** the falsifiers that would prove it wrong;
- **a fair range** of value, set against the last price;
- **the dates ahead,** such as earnings or a shareholders' meeting, which also appear on the calendar;
- **sections** on the business, its financials, valuation, catalysts and risks.

Ask for a **Deep analysis** under a listing's chart, and the agent writes a report and a forecast. It revises the report as you talk; each revision changes only what it names, and every earlier one is kept. Once a newer quarter comes out, the report is marked stale until the agent revises it, and no forecast is made before then.

## Claims keep their sources' words

A claim in a report carries a quote from its source. The quote must stand in something the conversation actually read, and a decisions model checks that it says what the claim asserts; a report that fails either is refused. [How quotes are checked](/docs/data/forecasts#quotes-must-be-read).

<Callout title="A decisions model makes the check">
  Without a [decisions model](/docs/setup/decisions), claims are kept but marked
  unread.
</Callout>

## The stance record

Each stance is measured against the market's index, from the revision that set it to the one that changed it. Once the stance has changed, a strip under it shows each stretch as wide as it ran, raised or lowered by how far the listing outran or trailed the index. [How excess return is measured](/docs/data/forecasts#stance-against-the-index).

## Falsifier watch

With a local embedder and a decisions model set up, Solyx reads the week's news against each falsifier after every collection, and tells the agent when one looks met. [How the watch works](/docs/data/forecasts#falsifier-watch).
