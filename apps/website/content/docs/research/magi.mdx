---
title: The MAGI
description: Three units, each a persona on a model you pick, that vote on forecasts and order proposals.
---

You choose who judges the agent's forecasts and proposals: the agent alone, or the MAGI. The MAGI put each of a forecast's scenarios and each order proposal to a vote. Three units, each a persona on a model you pick, judge alone:

| Unit                          | Weighs                                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **MELCHIOR·1**, the scientist | What the data and the evidence show                                            |
| **BALTHASAR·2**, the mother   | What could be lost, and whether standing aside is what the evidence called for |
| **CASPER·3**, the woman       | What the crowd wants now, and what standing aside gives up                     |

Two votes carry a motion. A carried order still waits for you.

## What is put to a vote

<Tabs items={["Order proposals", "Forecasts"]}>
  <Tab value="Order proposals">
    One motion, held only once the proposal has passed every risk check. A
    rejected order makes no proposal; a carried one waits for you to confirm it
    like any other.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Forecasts">
    Each scenario is its own motion: that the close is likelier to land in its
    band than its base rate says. A forecast without base rates is not voted on,
    and it is kept whatever the result.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The units never see the agent's own probabilities, direction or plan, only the evidence, so they judge the case rather than the conclusion. [What each unit reads](/docs/data/forecasts#the-magis-vote).

## Setting them up

Under **Settings → Models**, set **Decision mode** to **MAGI** and pick each unit's model, or leave a unit on the conversation's model. Each unit runs on your own key, so a vote costs three model calls.
