---
title: Themes
description: Topics that could reach what you hold over quarters, searched daily and marked by their signposts.
---

A theme watches a topic that could reach what you hold over quarters, such as a policy, a supply chain or a technology. It holds:

- **a thesis,** why the topic matters;
- **up to four queries,** which Solyx searches in the news every day through your web search key;
- **up to six signposts,** the developments that would mark it moving;
- **up to twelve listings** it could reach.

The decisions model reads each new item against each signpost, and an item it finds marks one becomes a development. A theme is quiet since its newest development. The agent can create and revise themes as you talk, and search one again, at most once an hour.
