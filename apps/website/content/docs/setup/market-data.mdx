Under **Settings → Market data**, **Taiwan market data** picks where charts and live bars for Taiwan listings come from: **Fugle**, on your own Fugle API key, or **Fubon Neo**, through your Fubon Securities account.

## Fugle

[Fugle](https://developer.fugle.tw/) serves Taiwan's quotes, candles and live bars. Its free Basic plan is enough to start.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Create a Fugle account** at [fugle.tw](https://www.fugle.tw/account/register).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Apply for a market data API key** on Fugle's [key page](https://developer.fugle.tw/docs/key/). Read the [terms of use](https://developer.fugle.tw/docs/data/intro#statement) first; applying for a key agrees to them.

    <Screenshot file="setup/fugle-api-key.png" alt="Fugle's key page, with a market data API key" />

  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Pick a plan if you need more.** Basic is free; Developer and Advanced are [paid subscriptions](https://developer.fugle.tw/docs/pricing) with more live subscriptions and requests.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Enter it in Solyx.** Under **Settings → Market data → Fugle**, set **API key**, and pick the **Plan** the key belongs to: **Basic (free)**, **Developer** or **Advanced**. Solyx paces its requests to that plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Plan      | Live subscriptions | Intraday requests / min | History requests / min |
| --------- | ------------------ | ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Basic     | 5                  | 60                      | 60                     |
| Developer | 300                | 600                     | 60                     |
| Advanced  | 2,000              | 2,000                   | 60                     |

<Callout title="Five subscriptions go quickly">
  On Basic, each chart's live bars take one subscription and its order book
  another, so a couple of charts and a watched exit fill the plan. A book the
  stream has no room for is read every 5 seconds instead.
</Callout>

## Fubon as market data

Pick **Fubon Neo** to read market data through your Fubon Securities account. It allows 300 live subscriptions, 300 intraday requests and 60 history requests a minute. Fubon's sign-in is set up under **Settings → Brokers**, and orders to a Fubon account use it too. [Setting up Fubon](/docs/setup/broker).

## FinMind

Statements, monthly revenue, dividends, investor flows and Taiwan's trading calendar come from [FinMind](https://finmindtrade.com/), which works without a token.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Register** at [finmindtrade.com](https://finmindtrade.com/) for a free account.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Copy your API token** from your [user page](https://finmindtrade.com/analysis/#/account/user).

    <Screenshot file="setup/finmind-token.png" alt="FinMind's user page with its API token" />

  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Enter it in Solyx.** Under **Settings → Market data → Fundamentals**, set **FinMind token** and pick its **Plan**: **Free**, **Backer**, **Sponsor** or **Sponsor Pro**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Plan        | Requests an hour |
| ----------- | ---------------- |
| No token    | 300              |
| Free        | 600              |
| Backer      | 1,600            |
| Sponsor     | 6,000            |
| Sponsor Pro | 20,000           |

Fundamentals, flows and the trading calendar share one budget.

## Open data

These sources need nothing from you.

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="TWSE and TPEx open data">
    From the [TWSE OpenAPI](https://openapi.twse.com.tw/) and [TPEx
    OpenAPI](https://www.tpex.org.tw/openapi/): company profiles, the exchanges'
    P/E, yield and price-to-book, price limits, sector closes, ex-dividend
    notices, shareholders' meetings, trading restrictions and warnings,
    governance notices (pledges, sanctions, fines, changes of control), material
    information, insider transfers, market closures and the funds TWSE lists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SITCA">
    Funds' daily net asset values and monthly costs, from [SITCA's open
    data](https://www.sitca.org.tw/), used under the [Open Government Data
    License, version 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/license).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Taiwan's statistics portal and DGBAS">
    The release calendar from [stat.gov.tw](https://www.stat.gov.tw/), and the
    figures of CPI, GDP, trade, export orders, industrial production and the
    business indicators from [DGBAS's statistical
    database](https://nstatdb.dgbas.gov.tw/).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="PTT's Stock board">
    Posts on [PTT's Stock board](https://www.ptt.cc/bbs/Stock/index.html) that
    name a listing you follow.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

<Callout type="info" title="Only what sites let programs read">
  Solyx reads no site whose terms forbid programs from downloading its data.
  TWSE's own websites do, so only what it publishes as open data is read; a
  fund's estimated net asset value, which only those sites hold, the agent finds
  on the issuer's own pages.
</Callout>
