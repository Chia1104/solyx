A scheduled task sends the agent a message on its own, each run in a conversation of its own that spends your model's tokens. Runs go on while Solyx sits in the menu bar.

## Making a task

Under **Settings → Schedules → Scheduled tasks**, or by asking the agent, give a task a **Name**, **What to send** and **When**.

<Tabs items={["Every so often", "At a time of day", "When something changed"]}>
  <Tab value="Every so often">
    Every 30 minutes, hour, 2, 4, 6 or 12 hours, or day. A run counts from the
    later of when the task was saved and when it last ran.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="At a time of day">
    At a time on your clock, every day, on the days Taiwan trades or on the days
    the US trades. A time missed while Solyx was closed still runs within 12
    hours; an older one is skipped.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="When something changed">
    When a report's falsifier is met, a report's date has gone by, or a theme's
    signpost is met, across everything you hold and watch or the listings and
    themes you pick. Solyx looks every half hour and runs the task at most once
    in the interval you set.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Calls that must ask** picks **Wait for me**, the default, or **Allow what is judged harmless**. A scheduled run never runs in **Don't ask**. A task's runs are marked **Scheduled** among your conversations, and **Open its conversation** shows the latest. **Run now** runs one at once.

The `watchlist-upkeep` skill makes a good task: a round over the watchlist ending in a briefing.

## What the app collects

Above the tasks, **What the app collects** sets how often Solyx collects on its own, with no model:

| What                            | Default        |
| ------------------------------- | -------------- |
| News of what you hold and watch | Every 72 hours |
| News for your themes            | Every 24 hours |

Each can run every 6 or 12 hours, daily, every 3 or 7 days, or at a time of day, and **Collect now** runs it at once. [Web search](/docs/setup/web-search#staying-within-a-free-tier).
