### Lots

A board lot is 1,000 shares. An odd lot is 1 to 999 shares, and one order is either whole lots or an odd lot, never both. Odd lots must be limit orders.

### Costs

| Cost        | Rate                                                             |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Commission  | 0.1425% of the value, at least NT$20, rounded down to the dollar |
| Tax on sale | 0.3%, or 0.1% for ETFs, REITs and ETNs                           |

The order panel writes each order's commission and, on a sale, its tax beside its value.

### Settlement

Trades settle with your bank on the second trading day after (T+2). What you can spend on buys is the settlement bank's balance with every trade already made settled against it: earlier days' trades as the broker reckoned them, and today's fills and buys still on the book with the most commission and tax taken off, so it errs low.

### Price limits

The limit band is read from the exchanges' own figures for each session (TWSE and TPEx), not worked out as ±10%, and it applies only to the session after the one it was published for. A listing whose published limit-up is 9,999.95 has no limit. A market order is sized at the limit-up price, the most it could fill at, so it is refused while the band is not known.

## US

The tick is 0.0001 below one dollar and 0.01 from there. Orders are whole shares.

## Limits Solyx sets

| Limit                             | Value                                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Largest order                     | NT$1,000,000 or US$30,000                                    |
| The paper account's starting cash | NT$1,000,000 and US$30,000                                   |
| Sessions a live account trades    | The regular session                                          |
| Sessions the paper account trades | Every session                                                |
| When a proposal goes stale        | Once its session has passed, or the price moved more than 3% |
| Longest an exit is watched        | 60 days                                                      |
