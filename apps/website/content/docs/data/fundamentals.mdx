The app works out every growth rate, margin and multiple itself, so the agent quotes a figure rather than computing it in its head. Amounts the agent reads are in millions, percentages to one decimal place and changes to two, with a missing figure written as n/a.

## Statements

FinMind supplies the statements, monthly revenue and dividends.

| What                          | How far back | Kept for |
| ----------------------------- | ------------ | -------- |
| Income statements             | 5 years      | 12 hours |
| Balance sheets and cash flows | 3 years      | 12 hours |
| Monthly revenue               | 36 months    | 12 hours |
| Dividends                     | 5 years      | 12 hours |

### Cleaning the rows

- A quarter without revenue is dropped, and so is a balance sheet without total assets.
- Net income is the profit attributable to the parent's owners, or after-tax income where that line is missing.
- Cash flows are filed year to date, so each quarter is its year-to-date figure less the quarter before; the first quarter is taken as filed. A quarter whose predecessor is missing has no figure.
- Capital spending is the cash paid for property, plant and equipment.
- A distribution's cash dividend adds its earnings and statutory surplus parts; so does its stock dividend. One that pays nothing is dropped.

### When a figure became public

Each quarter is taken as known from its filing deadline, so a past multiple uses only what had been published by that day.

| Quarter         | Deadline                   |
| --------------- | -------------------------- |
| Q1              | May 15                     |
| Q2              | August 14                  |
| Q3              | November 14                |
| Q4              | March 31, next year        |
| Monthly revenue | The 10th of the next month |

## Derived figures

| Figure                        | How it is worked out                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Growth                        | $$\frac{\text{now} - \text{before}}{\lvert \text{before} \rvert}$$, so a loss turning to profit reads as a rise |
| Revenue and EPS, year on year | Against the quarter twelve months earlier                                                                       |
| Revenue, quarter on quarter   | Against the quarter before                                                                                      |
| Monthly revenue               | Against the same month a year earlier, and the month before                                                     |
| Gross, operating, net margin  | The line ÷ revenue                                                                                              |
| Trailing EPS                  | The last four quarters added up; none if one is missing                                                         |
| Current ratio                 | Current assets ÷ current liabilities                                                                            |
| Liabilities to assets         | Total liabilities ÷ total assets                                                                                |
| Free cash flow                | Operating cash flow − capital spending, per quarter and over four quarters                                      |
| Trailing cash dividend        | Cash paid with an ex-dividend day in the past year                                                              |
| P/E now                       | The latest weekly close ÷ the newest trailing EPS                                                               |
| P/E history                   | Each weekly close over three years ÷ the trailing EPS public by then, as its low, median and high               |

P/E history leaves out weeks with trailing EPS at or below zero or fewer than four public quarters. Growth and margins are empty where the figure before is zero or missing.

The exchanges' own P/E, dividend yield and price-to-book come from TWSE and TPEx as they publish them; Solyx does not recompute them.

### Peers

A listing's peers are the others in its industry. For each multiple, Solyx gives the peers' median and the share of peers below the listing.

### The company

- **Market value** is the shares outstanding × the last finished session's close.
- **Turnover** is that session's volume ÷ shares outstanding.
- **Governance** notes list directors' pledged shares, then sanctions, exchange fines and changes of control.

## Investor flows

Flows (籌碼) show who trades a Taiwan listing: foreign investors, investment trusts, dealers and dealers' hedging. FinMind's foreign dealers count as foreign investors.

| Figure          | How it is worked out                                                                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Net buying      | Bought − sold, over the newest session, week (5 sessions) and month (20)             |
| Streak          | Sessions in a row on the newest session's side; positive for buying                  |
| Balance trend   | The newest margin, short or foreign holding less its value 1, 5 and 20 sessions back |
| Margin use      | Margin balance ÷ margin limit                                                        |
| Short to margin | Short balance ÷ margin balance                                                       |
| Futures         | Each group's long − short open interest in TAIEX futures                             |

Listing figures are given in lots, market trades in NT$ millions and foreign holding in percent. A week or month the data only partly covers reads as unknown on the chart. Flows are published between 15:00 and 21:00 Taipei time, so an answer is kept until FinMind's next publication, at 15:30, 18:30 or 21:30, and at most a day. A year of flows is read.

## Economic releases

Taiwan's release calendar comes from its statistics portal (stat.gov.tw), and the figures from DGBAS's statistical database.

| Release               | Agency                       | Figures from DGBAS                |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| CPI                   | DGBAS                        | CPI and core CPI, index           |
| Advance GDP           | DGBAS                        | GDP growth, %, quarterly          |
| Economic forecast     | DGBAS                        | None                              |
| Trade                 | Ministry of Finance          | Exports and imports, US$ millions |
| Industrial production | Ministry of Economic Affairs | Index                             |
| Export orders         | Ministry of Economic Affairs | US$ millions                      |
| Business indicators   | National Development Council | Signal score, points              |
| PMI                   | National Development Council | None                              |

- A release dated "by day N" is marked as a deadline; otherwise its day is set.
- A figure is matched to the newest reading for a period no later than the release's.
- Year-on-year change is worked out for indices and amounts, from the same period a year earlier; rates and scores get none.
- A period not yet out comes back as 0, so each series ends at its last real figure, and a table whose items change names fails rather than passing off another figure.
- The schedule is kept 12 hours and figures an hour. The agent reads the last 13 periods.

### US releases [#us-economic-releases]

While you are signed in to Longbridge, the US's releases and figures come from its macro data, each with what the market expected. Longbridge schedules a release only a few weeks ahead, so later ones are not on the calendar yet.

| Release                | Figures                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rate decision          | The middle of the Fed's target range, %                       |
| CPI                    | CPI and core CPI, year on year                                |
| Personal income        | PCE and core PCE prices, year on year                         |
| Employment report      | Payrolls added (thousands), unemployment, hourly earnings y/y |
| Retail sales           | Month on month                                                |
| ISM manufacturing      | Index                                                         |
| ISM services           | Index                                                         |
| Advance GDP            | Annualized, quarter on quarter                                |
| PPI                    | Year on year                                                  |
| Initial jobless claims | Thousands, by the week's last day                             |
| Consumer sentiment     | University of Michigan's preliminary index                    |

- Each figure shows the market's consensus beside it, and the next period's once its release is scheduled.
- A rate decision's period is the day it was announced.
- The overview's US Market tab also shows the VIX, and the Treasury's 2- and 10-year yields with the spread between them; the agent reads the last 10 days of yields with the US's figures.

## The calendar

The overview's calendar gathers, for the next 90 days:

- **Filings,** quarterly reports by their deadline and monthly revenue by the 10th, for Taiwan listings;
- **Distributions,** their ex-dividend, payment and ex-rights days, and the exchanges' own ex notices where a distribution gives no day;
- **Shareholders' meetings,** regular or special, and whether directors are elected;
- **Trading restrictions,** from their first day, or today while in force;
- **Market closures** and **economic releases,** which stay listed for seven days after their day;
- **Events in your reports.**

Each event is set, due by a deadline, or expected. The agent reads 30 days ahead unless it asks for up to 90.
