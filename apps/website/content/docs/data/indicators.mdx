title: Indicators
description: The formulas behind every line and pane on the chart, and what the agent reads.
---

Every indicator is worked out on your computer by the same code for the chart and for the agent, on bars [as traded](/docs/data/market-data#as-traded-or-adjusted). A line is empty for the first bars until its window is full.

## Which chart shows what

| Indicator                                                       | Where it is drawn           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Moving averages, Bollinger bands                                | Every interval              |
| VWAP, session levels                                            | Intraday bars only          |
| Relative strength, 52-week range, support zones, volume profile | Daily bars only             |
| MACD, RSI, KD, volume ratio                                     | Panes below the chart       |
| Investor flows                                                  | Taiwan listings, daily bars |

Short moving averages and KD are on until you turn them off. The panes stack in the order MACD, RSI, KD, volume ratio, relative strength.

## Moving averages

Simple averages of the close over Taiwan's usual periods:

$$
\text{SMA}_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} C_{t-k}
$$

| Short     | Long                                                                 |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5, 10, 20 | 60 (the quarter line), 120 (the half-year line), 240 (the year line) |

The long lines are drawn thicker. The exponential average the MACD uses starts from the simple average of its first $$n$$ closes and weights each close after it by $$\alpha$$:

$$
\text{EMA}_t = \alpha\, C_t + (1 - \alpha)\, \text{EMA}_{t-1}
\qquad
\alpha = \frac{2}{n + 1}
$$

## Bollinger bands

The 20-session simple average, with bands two standard deviations of the same 20 closes above and below it. The deviation is the population one, divided by $$n$$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{middle} &= \text{SMA}_{20}(C) \\
\text{upper} &= \text{middle} + 2\sigma_{20} \\
\text{lower} &= \text{middle} - 2\sigma_{20}
\end{aligned}
$$

## VWAP

The session's average price weighted by volume, starting over each exchange day:

$$
\text{VWAP} = \frac{\sum \frac{H + L + C}{3} \times V}{\sum V}
$$

It stays empty until the session has traded.

## RSI

Wilder's relative strength index over 14 sessions. The first 14 changes are averaged plainly, and each one after that is smoothed in:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{G}_t &= \frac{13\,\overline{G}_{t-1} + \text{gain}_t}{14}
\qquad
\overline{L}_t = \frac{13\,\overline{L}_{t-1} + \text{loss}_t}{14} \\
\text{RSI}_t &= 100 - \frac{100}{1 + \overline{G}_t / \overline{L}_t}
\end{aligned}
$$

With no losses RSI is 100; with neither gains nor losses it is 50. The pane rules lines at 30 and 70.

## MACD

12, 26 and 9, with Taiwan's names for the lines:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{DIF} &= \text{EMA}_{12}(C) - \text{EMA}_{26}(C) \\
\text{MACD} &= \text{EMA}_{9}(\text{DIF}) \\
\text{OSC} &= \text{DIF} - \text{MACD}
\end{aligned}
$$

The histogram is the plain difference, not doubled.

## KD

Taiwan's stochastic over 9 sessions, smoothed by thirds (9-3-3) rather than by the simple averages of the Western stochastic:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{RSV}_t &= \frac{C_t - L_9}{H_9 - L_9} \times 100 \\
K_t &= \tfrac{2}{3} K_{t-1} + \tfrac{1}{3} \text{RSV}_t \\
D_t &= \tfrac{2}{3} D_{t-1} + \tfrac{1}{3} K_t
\end{aligned}
$$

where $$H_9$$ and $$L_9$$ are the highest high and lowest low of the last 9 sessions.

K and D start at 50, and RSV is 50 when the window's high and low are equal. The pane rules lines at 20 and 80.

## Volume ratio

The bar's volume against its 20-bar average, $$V \div \text{SMA}_{20}(V)$$. The pane marks a surge at 1.5 or more and a lull under 0.5, with guide lines at 1 and 1.5.

## Relative strength

How far a listing has outrun its market's index over 20, 60 and 120 sessions, in percentage points:

$$
\text{RS}_n = \left( \frac{P_t}{P_{t-n}} - \frac{B_t}{B_{t-n}} \right) \times 100
$$

where $$P$$ is the listing's close and $$B$$ the benchmark's.

The benchmark is the TAIEX (`IX0001`) for a Taiwan listing and SPY for a US one, matched bar by bar by time.

## What the agent reads

The agent's indicator tool reports the same figures the chart draws: moving averages of 5, 10, 20, 60, 120 and 240 sessions, EMA 12 and 26, RSI 14, MACD 12/26/9, KD 9, Bollinger 20/2 and the volume ratio, together with the [levels](/docs/data/levels). The model cites them rather than working them out.
