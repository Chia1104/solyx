---
title: How Solyx computes
description: Where every figure on screen comes from, and the rules the app works it out by.
---

Every figure Solyx shows or the agent cites is worked out on your computer, by the app's own code, from data it reads straight from each source.

- **The app computes; the model quotes.** Indicators, levels, growth, margins, multiples, flows and base rates are worked out by code, the same for the chart and for the agent, so a reply cites the number on your screen rather than one the model worked out itself.
- **Prices as traded.** Charts and indicators show what actually traded, gaps from dividends and splits included. Only comparisons across history, backtests and the rotation graph, adjust for them.
- **Closed sessions are kept.** What a provider has published about a finished session is cached on your computer and never downloaded twice; what is still live is read again.
- **Within your plan.** Requests are spaced to the limits of each key's plan, and live subscriptions are shared between every window that needs one.
