## Intervals

The interval button above the chart offers 1, 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes, days, weeks and months, and **Intraday**, which charts the newest session on minute bars around its reference price, with its average price and volume across the session's hours.

## Indicators

**Indicators** switches each one on or off. Your choices are kept for every chart.

| Group          | Indicators                                                                       | On                            |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Lines          | Short MAs (5, 10, 20), Long MAs (60, 120, 240), BOLL                             | Every interval                |
| Session        | VWAP, Session levels                                                             | Intraday bars                 |
| Levels         | 52-week range, Support zones, Volume profile                                     | Daily bars                    |
| Panes          | MACD, RSI, KD, Volume ratio                                                      | Every interval                |
| Strength       | Relative strength                                                                | Daily bars                    |
| Investor flows | Foreign, trust and dealer net buying, margin and short balances, foreign holding | Taiwan, daily and longer bars |

Short MAs and KD are on until you turn them off. The long averages also mark where a close broke below the quarter or year line. [Every formula](/docs/data/indicators) and [how levels are found](/docs/data/levels).

## What the account draws

Your average cost, working orders and exits are drawn in ink, proposals still waiting in pencil, the session's limits, and an arrow on each bar where you bought or sold. [The order panel](/docs/orders/order-panel).

## Forecasts

On daily bars, a forecast's scenarios are drawn as dashed paths from its anchor, fainter the less likely, with its entry, stop and target. [Forecasts](/docs/research/forecasts).

## Filed figures

Under **Research**, **Filed figures** charts quarterly revenue, EPS, monthly revenue on the year (unaudited) and the gross, operating and net margins.

## Sector rotation

The overview's **Market** tab and a listing's research draw the rotation graph: where each sector or listing stands against a benchmark, and which way it is heading.

<Screenshot
  file="charts/rotation.png"
  alt="The sector rotation graph with its quadrants, trails and timeline"
/>

- **Bars** reads sessions or weeks, and **Period** how many: 20, 60 or 120 sessions, or 13, 26 or 52 weeks.
- **Groups** switch electronics, finance and other industries, or positions and watchlist, on and off.
- **Pins.** Pointing at an entry lifts its trail over the rest and names it. Click a dot, or a row in a quadrant's list, to pin it so it stays lifted while you point at others. **Unpin all** clears them.
- **As of** plays the graph back along a timeline, over up to the last 120 sessions, with **Play**, the previous and next session, and **Back to latest**.
- **Benchmark,** on the sector graph, takes another TWSE code in place of the TAIEX.

[How the graph is worked out](/docs/data/rotation).

## Order book

**Order book** shows the session's range between its limits, the best five bids and asks, the trial match while the auction runs, and your holding. Click a price to use it in the order panel.
