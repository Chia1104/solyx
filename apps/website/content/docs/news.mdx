Solyx collects the news of what you hold and watch, groups reprints and rewordings of one event into a story, and has a decisions model read who speaks in each and which way it leans.

## Sources

- **Material information** Taiwan companies file, and the share transfers their insiders declare.
- **PTT's Stock board.**
- **News outlets' articles, and Threads and X posts,** through your [web search key](/docs/setup/web-search).

Without a web search key, only filings, insider transfers and PTT are read.

## On a listing's page

**News and posts**, under the chart, lists the listing's stories by channel: filings, news, the forum and social posts. Beside them the **Sentiment** gauge reads from 0 to 100, 50 neutral, for the press and the crowd apart, with **Sentiment by day** below it. It says when the news was last collected and which sources failed.

## On the overview

**Headlines** on the overview's **News** tab ranks the past week's stories across what you hold and watch, weighting what the company says above outlets and outlets above investors, and earnings above market chatter.

## How often, and for how long

| Setting                                 | Default        | Where                                        |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Collect news of what you hold and watch | Every 72 hours | Settings → Schedules → What the app collects |
| Collect news for themes                 | Every 24 hours | Settings → Schedules → What the app collects |
| Keep press news                         | 90 days        | Settings → Storage                           |
| Keep crowd posts                        | 30 days        | Settings → Storage                           |

Collection can run every 6 or 12 hours, daily, every 3 or 7 days, or at a time of day; **Collect now** runs it at once. News can be kept 30, 90 or 180 days, a year, or forever.
