title: Orders
description: The one road every order takes, and the checks along it.
---

Every order in Solyx takes the same road. No setting, mode or extension adds another.

1. **The agent proposes.** Its tools can create a proposal and nothing else; you can also make one yourself in the [order panel](#the-order-panel).
2. **Risk checks run** against the account, the session and the market's rules.
3. **You confirm** the proposal in the app by holding **Hold to submit** until it fills, or your [trading mandate](#trading-mandate) covers it. Letting go sooner sends nothing. Only your hold, or a mandate you set yourself, reaches this step.
4. **The checks run again,** since the market and the account may have moved since the proposal was made.
5. **The order reaches the broker.**

A proposal that fails a check is rejected and says which rule it broke. One that waits stays pencilled in, hatched and dashed, until you confirm or dismiss it.

## The order panel

The right-hand pane has four tabs: **Chat** with the agent, with its conversations; **Orders**, the proposals waiting for you, the orders still working, the newest fills and past proposals; **Trade**, the order panel; and **P&L**, each currency's unrealized gain beside its cash, every holding marked at its last price, and what closed positions realized, as a running total with each trip under it.

On a listing's page it trades that listing; anywhere else, type a symbol and pick its market. It opens once the market's data source lists the symbol, so a mistyped one proposes nothing. The price follows the last trade until you set one: step it a tick at a time, pick a price from the book beside the chart, or take the limit down, the reference, the last trade or the limit up. A Taiwan order counts board lots, or shares for odd lots. As you fill it in, the order is written out in pencil with its value, commission and, on a sale, tax. **Check order** makes a proposal from it, which waits right below to confirm, and under **Orders** like any other. Changing the panel afterwards dismisses it.

The chart shows what the account does in the listing: your average cost and the orders still working in ink, stops and targets the broker watches dotted, proposals still waiting dashed in pencil, the session's limits, and an arrow on each bar where you bought or sold. **Pick on chart**, the crosshair above the chart, takes the price of your next click on it. **Intraday**, with the intervals under the button that names the bars shown, charts the newest session on minute bars, shaded above and below its reference price, with its average price and volume across the session's hours.

Beside the chart, **Order book**, the bars above it, shows the session's figures with where it has traded between its limits, the best five bids and asks and what you hold of the listing, marked at the last price. **Orders** under the chart lists the listing's orders still working and its newest fills. **Sell** or **Stop and target** on what you hold opens the order panel filled in with what you can sell, here, under **P&L** or on the overview. Nothing is proposed until you check it.

## The checks

- **Quantity.** A Taiwan order is 1 to 999 odd-lot shares or a multiple of a 1,000-share board lot; a US order is a whole number of shares.
- **Price.** A limit price sits on the listing's tick size and inside the day's limit band.
- **Size.** The order's value stays under your limit and within the account's cash, and a sale within the shares held.
- **Session.** The market's current session accepts orders, and the account trades that market.

Odd-lot orders must be limit orders. A market order is sized at the session's limit-up price, the most it could fill at, so it is rejected while that price is not known.

While you have set a daily loss limit, buys are refused once the day's sales have lost it, or while the broker has yet to reckon one of the day's sales. Sales still go.

The lock in the title bar refuses every order while it is on, those already waiting included, until you lift it.

## Failed submissions are never retried

If a submission fails, the broker may have accepted the order anyway, so Solyx marks it failed and never sends it again. The same holds for an order still submitting when the app quit. Every order carries a tag from its proposal, so Solyx looks for it at the broker: one the broker holds is marked submitted, and one it does not list once its session is over was never placed. You can ask it to look again from the proposal.

## Trading mandate

A trading mandate lets the agent's proposals go to the broker without waiting for you, within bounds you set in **Settings → Trading**:

- the listings it covers, and whether it covers buys or only sales;
- the most one order may come to, and the most a day's orders may come to and number;
- how many days it lasts, up to 30.

It covers limit orders only, never your own proposals, and only the account it was set for. Every check still runs, and confirming stays yours for anything outside it.

A new mandate is in **shadow mode** unless you switch it off: it marks each proposal it would have sent, and you still confirm them. Turn shadow mode off once its picks look right. If an order it sent goes unanswered by the broker, or Solyx closes while sending it, Solyx locks orders until you look at the account.

The mandate is sealed with your keys, so only you can set it, in the app. The agent cannot change it, and neither can an edit to Solyx's files.

## Stops and targets

To sell shares you hold once the price falls or rises far enough, set a stop, a target or both under **Stop and target** in the order panel, watched for up to 60 days. Like any proposal, it waits for you to confirm it. Fubon then watches the price itself, so it fires even while Solyx is closed: a stop sells at the day's lower limit, filling at what buyers bid, and a target sells at its price. Once one sells, Solyx cancels the other the next time it runs, so while Solyx is closed both could sell if you hold more shares than the exit sells. The paper account watches only while Solyx runs: it follows the live minute bars of each listing with an exit, which share your plan's live subscriptions with charts, and checks a listing the stream doesn't cover once a minute. You can cancel an exit from its card while it is still watched.

## Paper by default

Orders go to a paper account, so no real money moves. To trade your own account, set up Fubon's sign-in under **Settings → Brokers**, then pick your Fubon account under **Settings → Trading** and restart Solyx. Proposals still waiting for the paper account are dismissed then. Fubon reads the balance only of a settlement account at Taipei Fubon Bank or LINE Bank; with another bank, Solyx cannot tell what you can spend, so it refuses buys and only sales go. You can cancel an order that is still working under **Orders**.
