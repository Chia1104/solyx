## Reports

A report holds a view of a listing over quarters: its drivers, its risks, what would prove it wrong and the event ahead. Every revision is kept, and each stance is measured against the market's index.

A claim in a report carries its source's own words. The quote must stand in something the conversation actually read, and a decisions model checks that it says what the claim asserts; a report that fails either is refused.

## Forecasts

A forecast splits the horizon's close into price bands, each with a probability. Once made it is frozen, and when the horizon passes it is judged against what the price did and against how often the listing's own history lands in each band. Beside the record, a calibration chart shows how often the bands given each range of probability came true: on its diagonal, as often as given.

## The MAGI

You choose who judges the agent's forecasts and proposals: the agent alone, or the MAGI. The MAGI put each of a forecast's scenarios and each order proposal to a vote. Three units, each a persona on a model you pick, judge alone:

- **MELCHIOR·1, the scientist:** what the data and the evidence show.
- **BALTHASAR·2, the mother:** what could be lost, and whether standing aside is what the evidence called for.
- **CASPER·3, the woman:** what the crowd wants now, and what standing aside gives up.

Two votes carry a motion. A carried order still waits for you.
