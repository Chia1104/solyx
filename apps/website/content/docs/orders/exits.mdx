---
title: Stops and targets
description: Sell shares you hold once the price falls to a stop or rises to a target.
---

To sell shares you hold once the price falls or rises far enough, set a stop, a target or both under **Stop and target** in the order panel, watched for up to 60 days. Like any proposal, it waits for you to confirm it; a [trading mandate](/docs/orders/mandate) never sends one.

## Who watches the price

<Tabs items={["Fubon", "Paper account"]}>
  <Tab value="Fubon">
    Fubon watches the price itself, so an exit fires even while Solyx is closed: a stop sells at the day's lower limit, filling at what buyers bid, and a target sells at its price.

    Once one sells, Solyx cancels the other the next time it runs. While Solyx is closed, both could sell if you hold more shares than the exit sells.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Paper account">
    The paper account watches only while Solyx runs. It follows the live minute bars of each listing with an exit, which share your plan's live subscriptions with charts, and checks a listing the stream doesn't cover once a minute. A bar that reaches a leg sells the exit whole at the leg's price, or at the bar's open where it opened past it; a bar that reaches both sells at the stop, since nothing in it tells which came first.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can cancel an exit from its card while it is still watched.
