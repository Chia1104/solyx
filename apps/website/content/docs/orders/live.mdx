## Paper by default

Orders go to a paper account, so no real money moves. It starts with NT$1,000,000 and US$30,000 and takes orders in every session, so you can try the whole road after the close.

<Callout title="Paper fills are not a backtest">
  The paper account fills every order at once and in full, with no commission,
  tax or short selling. It is there to exercise the road to an order, not to
  judge a strategy; for that, ask the agent for a
  [backtest](/docs/data/backtests).
</Callout>

## Trading your own account

<Callout type="warn" title="Real money moves">
  Once you switch, a confirmed proposal, or one your trading mandate covers, is
  a real order at your broker.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Set up Fubon's sign-in** under **Settings → Brokers**: its SDK, your
    certificate and your login. [Setting up Fubon](/docs/setup/broker).
  </Step>
  <Step>**Pick your Fubon account** under **Settings → Orders**.</Step>
  <Step>
    **Restart Solyx.** Proposals still waiting for the paper account are
    dismissed then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A live account sends orders only to the regular session. You can cancel an order that is still working under **Orders**.

## Settlement banks

Fubon reads the balance only of a settlement account at Taipei Fubon Bank or LINE Bank. With another bank, Solyx cannot tell what you can spend, so it refuses buys and only sales go.
