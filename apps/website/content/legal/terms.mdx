## Your orders

Every order Solyx sends to a broker is yours.

- **You confirm, or your mandate sends.** An order reaches your broker only when you confirm it in the app, or when it falls within a trading mandate you set yourself. A mandate sends orders without asking you each time; you choose its bounds and remain responsible for what it sends. Stops and targets you confirm are watched and sold by your broker even while Solyx is closed.
- **Paper by default.** Solyx trades a paper account until you connect your own broker account. Once you do, confirmed orders are real orders at your broker, under your agreement with that broker.
- **Checks are not guarantees.** Solyx's risk checks, the order lock and the daily loss limit reduce some mistakes; they do not prevent every loss, and they depend on data from your broker and market data sources, which can be late or wrong.
- **Failed submissions.** If a submission fails, the broker may still have accepted the order. Solyx never sends it again, and you should check your account with your broker.
- **Your obligations.** You are responsible for following the laws and exchange rules that apply to you, your broker's terms (including any API agreement you signed with your broker) and your taxes.
