# `@solyx/fundamentals`

Fundamentals providers, one module per provider, each implementing the contracts of `@solyx/core/fundamentals` it serves. `./finmind` is a `FundamentalsProvider`: it reads Taiwan listings' quarterly income statements, balance sheets and cash flows, monthly revenue and dividends, who trades them and the market (`FlowsProvider` from `@solyx/core/flows`), and the days Taiwan's exchange trades (`TradingCalendarProvider` from `@solyx/core/session`), from FinMind's open API, which answers without a token under the lowest limit and with the user's token under the limit of the plan it belongs to (`FINMIND_PLANS`). The token and the plan are read for every request, so a saved one applies at once. `./tw-exchanges` is an `ExchangeListsProvider`: it reads the restrictions the Taiwan Stock Exchange and the Taipei Exchange list for their listings, the multiples they work out for them and the funds the Taiwan Stock Exchange lists, from the exchanges' open data, which needs no key. The Taipei Exchange publishes no list of its funds, so a fund listed there has no profile.

## Boundaries

- Normalize here to the shapes `@solyx/core` sets: a quarter without revenue is dropped, as is a balance sheet without total assets, a cash flow line a provider gives from the year's start is taken down to the quarter alone against the quarter before, and left empty where that one does not report it, flows count shares, not board lots, and the rows a provider splits one investor group into add up to one. A provider that gives no filing date stamps `knownFrom` with the market's filing deadline, so a past multiple never leans on results the market had not seen. A distribution that pays neither cash nor stock, such as a cash capital increase alone, is dropped, and one no announcement carries, as a fund's, keeps `announced` empty and takes its place from the provider's own date for it.
- A row that does not read is dropped, but an answer none of whose rows read fails: that is the provider writing the dataset another way, never a listing with nothing filed.
- Each module keeps within its provider's request limit with `@solyx/utils/rate-limit`, so callers keep one instance.
- A row of another listing is dropped, since a FinMind dataset may answer for every listing at once.
- An exchange's list is its own of today for the whole market: `./tw-exchanges` asks for each once per read and leaves how often to its caller, since the exchanges publish no limit, and reads nothing the lists no longer hold. A restriction keeps the exchange's own words as its note, and ends the day before the one an exchange names for lifting it. A list that answers one row with no code has nothing on it. A fund needs only its code, name and kind to read; any other field the exchange leaves out, or fills with a word for nothing, is empty. A multiple an exchange works none out for is empty too, and one written any other way drops its row.
