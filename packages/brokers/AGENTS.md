# `@solyx/brokers`

Brokers, one module per broker, each implementing `BrokerAdapter` from `@solyx/core/broker`. `./paper` is the paper broker; `./fubon` signs in to Fubon through the user's own SDK, exchanges the session for market data tokens and reads the stock account.

## Boundaries

- `./fubon`'s session runs only in the utility process the desktop starts for it.
- A broker throws `OrderRefusedError` only where it surely holds nothing of the order; any other failure leaves the outcome unknown, and the desk never sends that order again.
- `./paper` keeps its account and every fill in a `PaperLedger`, which the app backs with `@solyx/db/user`, so both outlive the app as proposals do. It writes the account and the fill last, in one write, so an order it cannot fill, for want of cash, shares or a price, is refused. It fills each order whole as it takes it, so its orders are its fills, each kept with the order's tag and limit price. It keeps an exit's legs in the ledger and checks them only as it is asked what it watches, against the listing's last price then: a leg that price has reached sells the exit whole at it and drops its siblings in the same write, and one past its last day ends unsold, so it watches nothing while the app is closed.
- `./fubon` loads `fubon-neo` at runtime from the path the user chooses; it is never a dependency or bundled. It covers sign-in, market data tokens and the stock account the user picked, which every call names by its `<branch>-<account>`, so none reads or trades another: holdings, orders and fills, placing and cancelling, and Fubon's reports of the account's orders being placed, changed or filled, which the session passes to one listener. An order is refused only where Fubon shows it failed (status 90): Fubon answers a refusal with its reason alone, so the order is looked up among the day's by its tag, and where none is listed, or one it has yet to number, the outcome stays unknown for the desk to find by its tag. Every SDK answer is parsed at the boundary, account queries are paced to Fubon's five a second, and orders and fills reach back `FUBON_HISTORY_DAYS`, read in Fubon's 30-day spans. It reads cash orders and holdings alone, since the app's orders neither borrow nor lend shares, and an order Fubon has yet to number, which it lists with a blank number, is left out until it has one. A holding's sellable shares are Fubon's tradable quantities of board and odd lots, which its working sales hold back from. An order reads at what it stands for now, so one cut down outside the app reads at its new size, and one Fubon's history lists as filled, without what filled, filled whole. What the account can spend is its bank's available balance with the earlier days' settlements Fubon reckoned, of which a settlement due today counts only what it takes out, and today's fills and working buys; Fubon reads the balance only of Taipei Fubon Bank and LINE Bank, so with another bank it is unknown. A sale is reckoned only where Fubon's report of gains lists it, and an account it keeps no such report for, as in its test environment, has none reckoned. An exit's legs are Fubon's condition orders, one for each price, watching the listing's own traded price: a stop sells at the day's lower limit and a target at its price, each from the first day Fubon takes, today only from 08:30 to 16:30 on a weekday, and a start of today Fubon refuses, as on a holiday, is placed from the next day. A leg Fubon refuses takes off those placed before it, so an exit is placed whole or not at all. Fubon words a condition order rather than giving its fields and carries no tag on it, so the legs in force are read from its wording (`fubonWatchedCondition`), and anything but a cash sale at or past a traded price is not one of the app's. It reads the SDK's version from the chosen folder's manifest without loading the SDK; `FUBON_SDK_VERSIONS` lists the versions `scripts/fubon-sdk-check.ts` passed with, and only the Fubon SDK workflow adds one, once it passes, since Fubon offers only its newest version for download.
- `scripts/fubon-test-env.ts` reads one of Fubon's shared test accounts through `./fubon`, printing each of the SDK's answers, or why it gave none, beside what the app reads of them, and with `--sell` places a sale through the broker, lists it and cancels it, so a change to the module is checked against Fubon itself before it reaches a real account. The test environment keeps no reports of costs or gains, so its holdings cannot be read there. `scripts/fubon-sdk-types.ts` type-checks the module against a download's declarations, with the SDK's string enums read as strings, since the module passes their values; `scripts/fubon-sdk-check.ts` exits with a failure where the module no longer reads the test environment's answers, placing at the day's lower limit as Fubon quotes it, since the test environment's prices are its own, and has Fubon watch an exit at prices no session reaches, lists it and cancels it.
