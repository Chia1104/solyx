# `@solyx/brokers`

Brokers, one module per broker, each implementing `BrokerAdapter` from `@solyx/core/broker`. `./paper` is the paper broker; `./fubon` signs in to Fubon through the user's own SDK, exchanges the session for market data tokens and reads the stock account.

## Boundaries

- `./fubon`'s session runs only in the utility process the desktop starts for it.
- A broker throws `OrderRefusedError` only where it surely holds nothing of the order; any other failure leaves the outcome unknown, and the desk never sends that order again.
- `./paper` keeps its account and every fill in a `PaperLedger`, which the app backs with `@solyx/db/user`, so both outlive the app as proposals do. It writes the account and the fill last, in one write, so an order it cannot fill, for want of cash, shares or a price, is refused. It fills each order whole as it takes it, so its orders are its fills, each kept with the order's tag and limit price.
- `./fubon` loads `fubon-neo` at runtime from the path the user chooses; it is never a dependency or bundled. It covers sign-in, market data tokens and the stock account: holdings, orders and fills, placing and cancelling. An order is refused only where Fubon shows it failed (status 90): Fubon answers a refusal with its reason alone, so the order is looked up among the day's by its tag, and where none is listed, or one it has yet to number, the outcome stays unknown for the desk to find by its tag. Every SDK answer is parsed at the boundary, account queries are paced to Fubon's five a second, and orders and fills reach back `FUBON_HISTORY_DAYS`, read in Fubon's 30-day spans. It reads cash orders and holdings alone, since the app's orders neither borrow nor lend shares, and an order Fubon has yet to number, which it lists with a blank number, is left out until it has one. What the account can spend is its bank's available balance with the earlier days' settlements Fubon reckoned, of which a settlement due today counts only what it takes out, and today's fills and working buys; Fubon reads the balance only of Taipei Fubon Bank and LINE Bank, so with another bank it is unknown. A sale is reckoned only where Fubon's report of gains lists it, and an account it keeps no such report for, as in its test environment, has none reckoned.
- `scripts/fubon-test-env.ts` reads one of Fubon's shared test accounts through `./fubon`, printing each of the SDK's answers, or why it gave none, beside what the app reads of them, and with `--sell` places a sale through the broker, lists it and cancels it, so a change to the module is checked against Fubon itself before it reaches a real account. The test environment keeps no reports of costs or gains, so its holdings cannot be read there.
