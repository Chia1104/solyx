# `@solyx/brokers`

Brokers, one module per broker, each implementing `BrokerAdapter` from `@solyx/core/broker`: `./paper` is the paper broker, and `./fubon` trades the user's Fubon stock account through their own SDK and hands its session's market data tokens on.

## Boundaries

- `./fubon`'s session runs only in the utility process the desktop starts for it.
- A broker throws `OrderRefusedError` only where it surely holds nothing of the order; any other failure leaves the outcome unknown, and the desk never sends that order again.
- `./paper` keeps its account and fills in a `PaperLedger`, which the app backs with `@solyx/db/user`, so both outlive the app as proposals do. It fills each order whole as it takes it, writing the account and the fill last in one write, so an order it cannot fill for want of cash, shares or a price is refused; its orders are its fills, each kept with the order's tag and limit price.
- `./paper` keeps an exit's legs in the ledger and, while the app runs, follows the live minute bars of each watched listing from the stream charts share, from the first bar that opens after the exit was placed and after this run began following the listing, so a price reached while the app was closed sells nothing. A bar that reaches a leg sells the exit whole at the leg's price, at the bar's open where it opened past it, or at the stop where it reached both, and drops the other legs in the same write. A listing no stream covers, as past the plan's symbols, is checked against its last price each time the desk asks what it watches, and an exit past its last day ends unsold then.
- `./fubon` loads `fubon-neo` at runtime from the path the user chooses; it is never a dependency or bundled. It reads the SDK's version from that folder's manifest without loading the SDK. `FUBON_SDK_VERSIONS` lists the versions `scripts/fubon-sdk-check.ts` passed with, and only the Fubon SDK workflow adds one, once it passes, since Fubon offers only its newest version for download.
- Every `./fubon` call names the account the user picked by its `<branch>-<account>`, so none reads or trades another. Every SDK answer is parsed at the boundary, account queries are paced to Fubon's five a second, and orders and fills reach back `FUBON_HISTORY_DAYS`, read in Fubon's 30-day spans.
- A Fubon order is refused only where Fubon shows it failed (status 90). Fubon answers a refusal with its reason alone, so the order is looked up among the day's by its tag, and where none is listed, or one Fubon has yet to number, the outcome stays unknown for the desk to find by its tag. An order Fubon lists with a blank number is left out until it has one.
- `./fubon` reads cash orders and holdings alone, since the app's orders neither borrow nor lend shares. A holding's sellable shares are Fubon's tradable board and odd lots, which its working sales hold back from. An order reads at what it stands for now, so one cut down outside the app reads at its new size, and one Fubon's history lists as filled, without what filled, filled whole.
- What a Fubon account can spend is its bank's available balance with the earlier days' settlements Fubon reckoned, of which a settlement due today counts only what it takes out, and today's fills and working buys; Fubon reads the balance only of Taipei Fubon Bank and LINE Bank, so with another bank it is unknown. A sale is reckoned only where Fubon's report of gains lists it, so an account without that report, as in the test environment, has none reckoned.
- An exit's legs at Fubon are condition orders, one per price, watching the listing's own traded price: a stop sells at the day's lower limit and a target at its price. Each runs from the first day Fubon takes, today only from 08:30 to 16:30 on a weekday; a start of today Fubon refuses, as on a holiday, is placed from the next day. Fubon answers a refusal and a failure it cannot vouch for alike, so a failed answer is a refusal only where Fubon then lists no such leg placed since, and a cancel only where its counts say the leg was cancelled. A leg Fubon refuses takes off those placed before it, so an exit is placed whole or not at all. Fubon words a condition order rather than giving its fields and tags none, so the legs in force are read from its wording (`fubonWatchedCondition`), and anything but a cash sale at or past a traded price is not one of the app's.
- Check a change to `./fubon` against one of Fubon's shared test accounts with `scripts/fubon-test-env.ts` (`--sell` places, lists and cancels a sale) before it reaches a real account; the test environment keeps no reports of costs or gains, so its holdings cannot be read there. `scripts/fubon-sdk-check.ts` fails where the module no longer reads the test environment's answers, placing at the day's lower limit as Fubon quotes it, since the test environment's prices are its own. `scripts/fubon-sdk-types.ts` type-checks the module against a download's declarations, with the SDK's string enums read as strings, since the module passes their values.
